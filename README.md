Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard – Power BI
📊 Project Overview

An interactive Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends.

The project transforms raw Amazon sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard with KPIs, charts, and dynamic filters.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
Identify top-selling products
Analyze sales by city and state
Identify high-value customers
Understand revenue distribution by gender
Track monthly revenue trends
Provide interactive filtering for business analysis

📈 Dashboard Insights
Monthly Revenue Trend

Analyzes revenue performance across months and years to identify sales patterns and trends.

Top 5 Products by Sales

Highlights the products generating the highest revenue.

Sales by City

Compares sales performance across different cities.

State-wise Orders & Revenue

Provides a comparison of order volume and revenue across states.

Top Customers by Spending

Identifies customers contributing the highest revenue.

Revenue by Gender

Shows the distribution of revenue between male and female customers.

🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes dynamic slicers for:

Prime Membership
Year
KYC Verification
Order Status

Changing the filters dynamically updates the dashboard KPIs and visualizations.
